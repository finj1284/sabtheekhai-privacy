🛡️ Privacy Policy

Sab Theek Hai — Guardian Safety Check-in App

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

Sab Theek Hai is a personal safety check-in application operated by FCJ Technologies (DBA Sab Theek Hai), registered in India. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at sabtheekhain.guardianapp@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

  • Name & Email — to create and manage your account
  • Phone number — for emergency contact setup
  • Precise GPS location — only when you actively send a check-in or SOS alert (not continuously)
  • Device ID (FCM token) — to send safety notifications to your device
  • Subscription history — to manage your active plan
  • Consent record — timestamp, language, and version of your consent (required by DPDP Act 2023)

⚠️ We do NOT collect location continuously. GPS is only accessed when you tap a check-in or SOS button. We do NOT sell your data to advertisers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Send your location to your chosen emergency contacts via Telegram when you trigger a check-in or SOS
  • Send safety notifications to your device
  • Manage your subscription and billing
  • Comply with India's DPDP Act 2023 and applicable laws

4. Data Retention

  • Check-in GPS coordinates: Auto-deleted after 30 days
  • Account data: Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Consent records: Retained for the life of your account
  • Audit logs: Retained for 2 years (legal requirement)

5. Who Sees Your Data

Only your chosen emergency contacts receive your location via Telegram when you actively trigger an alert. We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, push notifications
  • Telegram — message delivery to emergency contacts
  • Razorpay — payment processing (we never store card details)
  • Google Maps — location link generation

6. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

📥 Access Download all data we hold about you from Settings → My Data.
✏️ Correct Update your profile, phone number, or emergency contacts anytime.
🗑️ Erase Request full account deletion. Data wiped within 30 days.
👤 Nominate Designate someone to exercise your rights after your death.
🔒 Withdraw Consent Revoke consent anytime from Settings → My Data. Stops all processing immediately.
📋 Grievance File a complaint with our DPO. Response within 72 hours.

7. Children's Data

This app is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Age confirmation is required during registration as mandated by DPDP Act 2023 Section 9.

8. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Firebase Security Rules restrict access so only the account owner can read their data. We conduct periodic security reviews.

9. Cross-Border Transfers

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers. Firebase operates data centres in multiple regions. For users in Gulf countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar), your data may be stored in servers outside your country in compliance with applicable data transfer agreements.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we materially change this policy, you will be asked to review and re-consent within the app before continuing to use it. The consent version number will be incremented.
