About This Policy

Sab Theek Hain ("we", "our", "the app") is a family safety check-in application that allows users to notify their family members that they are safe via Telegram messages. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and your rights over your data.

We obtain your explicit, informed consent before collecting any personal data, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). You will be asked to review and actively agree to this policy before creating an account. If you do not agree, please do not use the app. You may withdraw your consent at any time from Settings → My Data → Withdraw Consent Withdraw consent — you may withdraw consent for location data collection at any time from Settings → My Data → Withdraw Consent, without deleting your account. Withdrawing consent disables location-based features. Account deletion — permanently delete your account and all data from Settings → Account → Delete Account. All data is purged within 30 days.

✅ We do not collect biometric data, face recognition data, or photos. The app uses a one-tap check-in button. No camera access is required.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes only:

  • Sending safety alerts — your name and check-in time are included in the Telegram message sent to your emergency contacts
  • Authentication — your email identifies your account and keeps it secure
  • Scheduling reminders — your timezone is used to fire reminders at the correct local time
  • Rate limiting — we track your last check-in time to prevent accidental spam (one alert per 5 minutes)
  • Subscription management — to know which features you have access to

⚠️ We never use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties. Ever.

🗄️

Where Your Data Is Stored

All user data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. Your data does not leave India's geographic boundary on our servers.

Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). Payment processing is handled by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. (an Indian company, RBI regulated, PCI DSS compliant).

Telegram messages are delivered via the Telegram Bot API. The only information sent to Telegram is your display name, check-in timestamp, and optional approximate location. No account credentials are shared with Telegram.

Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer
  • Withdraw consent — delete your account at any time in app Settings

✅ You can delete your account and all data directly from the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Data is permanently removed within 30 days.

👧

Children's Privacy

Sab Theek Hai is intended exclusively for users aged 18 years and above. We do not collect, process, or store personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP), a "child" means any person under the age of 18. We do not process children's data under any circumstance, including with parental consent, as our app is not designed or intended for minors. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, please contact privacy@sabtheekhai.com immediately and we will delete the account and all associated data within 72 hours.

  • No passwords stored — Firebase Authentication handles credentials securely
